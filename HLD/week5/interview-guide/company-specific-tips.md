# System Design Interview: Company-Specific Tips

## Google

**Focus Areas**:
- Massive scale (billions of users)
- Distributed systems
- Efficiency and cost optimization
- Innovation (novel approaches)

**Common Questions**:
- Design Google Search
- Design YouTube
- Design Gmail
- Design Google Maps
- Design Google Drive

**Interview Style**:
- Expect deep technical dives
- Scalability is paramount (think billions)
- May ask about Google technologies (Bigtable, Spanner, MapReduce)
- Algorithm optimization matters

**Preparation Tips**:
- Study Google's published papers (Bigtable, Spanner, MapReduce)
- Emphasize distributed systems knowledge
- Be ready to discuss data center efficiency
- Focus on algorithms and data structures

## Startup Culture Companies

**Focus Areas**:
- Move fast, iterate
- MVP mentality
- Cost consciousness
- Rapid scaling
- Simple over complex

**Interview Style**:
- Pragmatic solutions over perfect ones
- "What would you build for V1?"

**Preparation Tips**:
- Start simple, add complexity when needed
- Discuss MVP vs long-term
- Be practical about costs
- Show you can ship quickly

## General Tips Across Companies

**Research the company**:
- Read engineering blog
- Understand their tech stack
- Know their scale and challenges
- Study their products

**Tailor your examples**:
- Reference company's products
- Show you understand their problems
- Demonstrate cultural fit

**Ask informed questions**:
- About their architecture
- About their challenges
- About their tech choices

**Show enthusiasm**:
- Genuine interest in their problems
- Excitement about their mission
- Desire to contribute

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Bad-mouthing current/previous employer
- Not knowing basics about the company
- Generic answers not tailored to company
- Arrogance or dismissiveness
- Not acknowledging trade-offs
- Inflexibility in approach
- Poor communication
